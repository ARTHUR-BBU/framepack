# Product Launch Video

Build desire by making the product the protagonist. Show value before feature volume.

## Required inputs

- Product name and one-sentence promise.
- Three to five verified benefits.
- Real product image, capture, video, or interface evidence.
- Audience and call to action.
- Brand tokens or explicit permission to derive a temporary direction.

Mark missing inputs. Never invent screens, customers, metrics, or capabilities.

## Story rhythm

1. `hook` — expose the viewer's tension or desired outcome.
2. `proof` — show the real product resolving that tension.
3. `experience` — connect benefits through one continuous product experience.
4. `cta` — hold a simple promise and next action.

Use the strongest real asset in the hook or proof. Use text to clarify evidence, not replace it. Prefer crop, focus, comparison, annotation, and spatial continuity over a stack of generic cards.

## Direction checks

- Can the viewer name the product and benefit after five seconds?
- Is every claim supported by supplied material or user confirmation?
- Does the product occupy more visual authority than decoration?
- Does the final hold remain readable without motion?

## Runtime directives

```framepack-rules
{
  "rhythm": "hook-proof-experience-cta",
  "scenePurposes": ["hook", "proof", "experience", "cta"],
  "assetPurposes": ["proof", "experience"],
  "assetPriority": "product-first",
  "avoid": ["generic-card-stack", "unsupported-product-claims", "decoration-before-proof"]
}
```

## Common mistakes

- Opening with the company logo before establishing desire.
- Treating a screenshot as background decoration instead of evidence.
- Listing features at one visual intensity.
- Hiding a weak product story behind neon, glass, or excessive particles.
