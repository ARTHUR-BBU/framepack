# Intent & Intake Department

> Role: the front desk. Understand the job before the kitchen starts cooking.
>
> Plain-English job: Intent & Intake is the maître d'. It hears what the user wants, classifies the request, asks for the right ingredients, and decides which decisions need human checkpoints.

## 1. Department boundary

Intent & Intake owns **request routing and asset awareness**:

- Classify user intent: product launch, website-to-video, explainer, PR, captions, overlays, templates, reference mining.
- Ask for assets proactively: logo, screenshots, product pages, source video, BGM, brand palette, references.
- Decide which creative decisions require co-creation vs. autonomous progression.

Intent & Intake does **not** own:

- scene choreography — Director Bible owns that
- taste judgment — Taste Intelligence owns that
- weapon selection — Weapon Production owns that

## 2. Contracts

| Direction | Artifact | Consumer |
|---|---|---|
| Output | intent classification | Director Bible |
| Output | asset intake checklist | Director Bible, Audit |
| Output | user-gate decision points | Intervention |
| Output | workflow route | HyperFrames workflow skill |

## 3. Current status

Implemented and mature. Intent Router and Asset Intake have been stable since v0.14.
